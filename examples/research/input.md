# Research planning example

Synthetic case. All people, places and collections below are fictional.

Request: Use the supplied material to plan the next search for Mara Vale's
parents. Do not browse, contact anyone or change a tree. Return a short report
and a genealogy-handoff-1 JSON proposal.

- P1: Mara Vale, the research subject. A supplied family note S1, paragraph 1,
  says she was born "about 1880 in Northmere." No document confirms this.
- S2: a supplied search-log extract. On 2026-11-01, an earlier researcher read
  pages 1-2 of the fictional Northmere birth index for 1878-1882, found no match
  for "Mara Vale" and stopped because access failed. Pages 3-6 remain unread.
- S3: a supplied catalog extract says the birth index has six pages covering
  1878-1882. The original birth register and its entries are not supplied.

Only these texts are available. There is no live archive access in this example.
